# ✨ Customizable Greeting (`Customizable-Greeting.html`)

An all-in-one, single-file interactive birthday greeting experience—zero servers, zero installs, and zero backend needed. Just open `Customizable-Greeting.html` in a browser, make it personal in minutes, and export a ready-to-send offline `.html` surprise.

---

## 🌟 What Makes It Special

* **🌌 Living Starlit Sky:** Tap or click the open sky to send glowing wish lanterns drifting upward across a starfield with a crescent moon, shooting stars, fireflies, petals, and moonlit water reflections.
* **🎨 3 Instant Color Vibes:** Switch live between **Starlit Rose**, **Midnight Lavender**, and **Golden Sunset** to match the mood.
* **🏝️ Lantern Island Entry:** A fairy-lit island welcome scene with glowing birthday typography and instant lantern launches.
* **🌸 Garden, Cute Teddies & Sweet Treats:**
  * Click flowers to burst petals and watch butterflies flutter around.
  * Meet three illustrated plush teddy bears with blush/hug reactions and custom notes.
  * Browse an artisan chocolate, macaron, and cupcake spread with playful sweet wishes.
* **📸 Draggable & Flippable Polaroid Desk:** A warm wooden desk strung with fairy lights where polaroids can be dragged around and flipped in 3D to read hidden notes on the back.
* **💌 Wax-Sealed Secret Letter:** Break a crimson wax seal to unfold a full parchment birthday letter.
* **🎂 Interactive Cake & Fireworks Finale:** Click candles—or blow into your mic if enabled—to put them out and trigger fireworks, confetti, balloons, and wish lanterns.
* **🎵 Offline Music Box:** Built-in Web Audio API melody loop and magical chimes—no external MP3 links required.
* **🛠️ Live Customize & Export Studio:** Edit names, themes, teddy notes, letter text, and candle counts on the spot, upload photos (automatically resized and embedded inside the file), and download a clean standalone `.html` gift with the editor tucked away.

---

## 🚀 How to Use It

### 1. Open It Right Away
1. Download or grab `Customizable-Greeting.html`.
2. Double-click it to open in **Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave**, or any modern browser (also works on mobile browsers).

### 2. Personalize & Export a Gift File
1. Open `Customizable-Greeting.html` in your browser.
2. Click **✨ Customize & Export** in the top-right corner.
3. Fill in the recipient/sender names, pick a theme, customize the teddy notes, birthday letter, and candle count, and add photos from your device.
4. Click **Download Final Gift (.html)** to save a self-contained `.html` file you can send straight to someone.

---

## 💡 Good to Know

* **100% Self-Contained:** Photos are compressed and baked directly into the exported `.html` file so they don't break when shared offline.
* **Keep File Size Friendly:** Using a handful of compressed photos keeps the exported `.html` fast to load on phones as well as laptops.
* **Browser Mic Tip:** Blowing out cake candles via microphone requires browser mic permission (clicking the candles always works as a quick fallback).
